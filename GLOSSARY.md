# Salency

The words the marketing site uses for what Salency does. Page copy follows these terms. What ships is defined in `PRODUCT.md`; this file fixes the words, not the facts.

## People and accounts

**Account**:
A company your team is selling to or serving, followed across many calls and through changes of owner (AE to CSM, one rep to the next). The app lists accounts under "Prospects".
_Avoid_: prospect (except when naming the app screen), customer, client, deal

**Rep**:
Anyone on your team who works an account: AE, CSM, or SDR. Name the title only when the role matters ("AE to CSM").
_Avoid_: seller, salesperson, user, successor

**Contact**:
A person at an account who has appeared on a call, with the stance Salency inferred from what they said.
_Avoid_: stakeholder, buyer, prospect (for a person)

**Handoff**:
The moment an account moves from one rep to another. "Inherit" is fine as the verb for the rep receiving it. Never the name of a document.
_Avoid_: transition, inheritance event, rotation, handoff brief

## Calls

**Call**:
One conversation between your team and an account.
_Avoid_: meeting (app vocabulary), conversation, recording

**Transcript**:
The text of a call, made by your notetaker and uploaded to Salency. Salency reads transcripts; it never records calls.
_Avoid_: recording, call notes

**Signal**:
One thing Salency takes from a single call, backed by a citation. The kinds are pain, objection, requirement, competitor, next step, key event, and urgency. Contradictions and product matches are not signals.
_Avoid_: extraction, insight, data point

**Citation**:
The quote, who said it, and which call. Every signal and contradiction points back to one. "Cited" is fine as the verb.
_Avoid_: source, receipt, evidence, timestamp

**Pain**:
A signal: a problem the account has now or is worried about.
_Avoid_: pain point, problem, challenge

**Requirement**:
A signal: something the account explicitly said it needs or asked about. "They need" stays as a company brief heading.
_Avoid_: need, ask, must-have

**Next step**:
A signal: something either side agreed to do, as said on a call.
_Avoid_: action item

## Across calls

**Account memory**:
Everything Salency has gathered on one account across its calls: pains, objections, requirements, commitments, contacts, and what's changed.
_Avoid_: account graph, the graph, memory layer

**Commitment**:
Something your side has said it will do for an account and hasn't done yet. Part of account memory, not a signal.
_Avoid_: todo, promise, action item

**Contradiction**:
A later call saying something that doesn't agree with an earlier one, shown with both citations. "What's changed" is the company brief section that lists them.
_Avoid_: conflict, inconsistency, change delta

**Shift**:
A pain that changed between calls without contradicting what came before: a new scope, urgency, owner, or cadence.
_Avoid_: change delta, delta, evolution

## Your catalog

**Catalog**:
Your products and features as loaded into Salency from a URL, PDF, or CSV. "Product catalog" is fine where it reads better.
_Avoid_: solutions, offerings, product library

**Product match**:
One pain or requirement linked to a product or feature in your catalog, with a confidence tier. "Pain-to-product mapping" names the whole set.
_Avoid_: fit, recommendation, solution

**Confidence tier**:
How sure Salency is about a product match: Strong, Probable, or Speculative. Only product matches have one; signals never do. A numeric score appears only in the exported company brief.
_Avoid_: confidence score, ranking, certainty

## What a rep gets

**Company brief**:
The PDF a rep exports from Salency for one account: who's involved, what they need, what's owed, what's changed, and how it maps to the catalog.
_Avoid_: account brief, handoff brief, Day-1 brief, the brief (on its own)

**Follow-up draft**:
The email Salency writes after a call, subject and body, for the rep to edit and send themselves. Salency never sends it.
_Avoid_: follow-up (on its own), auto-reply, sequence, automated email

## Positioning and commercial

**Institutional memory**:
The category Salency belongs to. Used in positioning lines only, never for something a rep opens.
_Avoid_: using it for one account's memory

**Graph**:
The planned structure linking accounts, so patterns across them can be seen. Not shipped: the site mentions it only as a plan, never as something available today.
_Avoid_: using it for account memory

**Pilot**:
The free 4–6 week engagement where Salency runs on your team's real transcripts, with no obligation afterwards. Never given a date.
_Avoid_: trial, beta, early access, cohort
